# Teqvira — Frequently Asked Questions

## Q: How long does a typical project take?
A: Project timelines vary based on scope and complexity:
- Simple websites: 2-4 weeks
- Mobile apps: 6-12 weeks
- SaaS platforms: 3-6 months
- AI/custom software: Varies based on requirements
We provide a detailed timeline after the discovery call.

## Q: How do we communicate during the project?
A: We assign a dedicated project manager to every project. You'll receive:
- Weekly progress updates via email
- Access to a shared project management board (Notion/Jira)
- Bi-weekly video calls for demos and feedback
- Slack/WhatsApp channel for quick communication

## Q: Do you sign NDAs?
A: Yes, absolutely. We sign NDAs before any project discussion. Your IP and business ideas are fully protected.

## Q: Who owns the code and intellectual property?
A: You do. 100% of the codebase, design assets, and intellectual property are transferred to you upon project completion.

## Q: Do you provide post-launch support?
A: Yes. All projects include a 30-day post-launch support period at no extra cost. Extended support and maintenance packages are also available.

## Q: What payment methods do you accept?
A: We accept bank transfers, PayPal, Stripe, and cryptocurrency. Payment schedules are milestone-based.

## Q: Can you work with our existing team?
A: Yes, we can augment your team or work independently. We're flexible with collaboration styles.

## Q: Do you build MVPs for startups?
A: Yes, we have a dedicated MVP development track that's optimized for speed and cost-efficiency, designed to help startups validate their ideas quickly.

## Q: What industries do you serve?
A: We work across FinTech, HealthTech, EdTech, E-commerce, SaaS, Real Estate, Logistics, and more.

## Q: How do I get started?
A: Simply tell Govind (our AI assistant) what you're looking for, and we'll collect your requirements and schedule a discovery call with our team.
